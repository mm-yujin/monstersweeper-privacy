Privacy Policy — Monster Sweeper

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Monster Sweeper ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game published by HiCheeze ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own, and no server we control. How far you have got in each dungeon, and your language, sound, volume and vibration settings, are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What our advertising partner collects

The app shows optional rewarded video ads. You only ever see one if you ask for it: either by tapping "Continue" to rewind a run after dying, or by asking for a fresh set of blessings between two floors. There are no ads anywhere else in the game — no banners, and nothing that plays on its own. These ads are served by Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies.

To serve and measure those ads, Unity Ads may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • device and technical information such as model, operating system version, language and country
  • IP address, coarse location derived from it, and ad interaction events

This data is collected by Unity, not by us. We never receive it, and we cannot identify you from it. Unity's handling of it is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Ads are entirely optional — the game is fully playable without watching a single one, and no feature is locked behind them.

On Android you can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so does not affect the game.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.
